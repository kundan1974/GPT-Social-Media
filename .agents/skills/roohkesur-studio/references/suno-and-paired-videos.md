## Poem arrives

Deliver copy-ready fields, keeping the poem separate from interpretation and production directions:

1. A suggested song title and brief musical treatment.
2. Lyrics in a dedicated field with economical structural tags. Conventional examples include [Verse], [Chorus] and [Bridge]; use only structures the poem supports.
3. A separate style description covering genre, mood, soulful vocal character, clear Hindi/Urdu diction, tempo feel, instruments, arrangement, production and ending.
4. Separate pronunciation and performance notes, optional exclusions where the current interface supports them, and a short creation/listening checklist.
5. A proposed 30-second highlight concept; exact boundaries remain pending the recording.

Keep all supplied wording. Do not invent a chorus or sacrifice a poem's argument to a pop template. If repeating original lines as a refrain is musically useful, show that repetition as a proposed choice. Do not silently reorder the verse. Transliteration, translation or wording adaptation is separate and requires the user's direction.

Describe a soulful performance through warmth, intimacy, expressive phrasing, restrained ornamentation and clear diction. Choose a sound world suited to the poem: an acoustic ballad, a ghazal-influenced arrangement, ambient textures or another appropriate treatment. Do not use one obligatory genre for every poem. Keep detailed sonic direction in the style/notes fields; avoid large piles of speculative tags in the lyrics.

Tags steer generation; they do not guarantee voice, wording, section boundaries or duration. Verify current Suno fields and limits instead of hard-coding them. No working Suno generation connector is required: the creator generates the song and supplies the selected final result.

Return the package in reusable writing blocks or files supported by the host. State that it is ready for Suno and set awaiting_song. This is the intentional handoff, not an incomplete video render.

## Song returns

Treat the selected audio as timing authority. Preserve the original privately. Listen in full; log its version, format, duration, actual sung text and instrumental sections. Surface omissions or changed poem words before presenting them as faithful.

Select a 30-second passage that forms a complete thought and has an effective opening and ending. Do not cut through a word or a couplet merely to follow a beat. Record source in/out, edit duration and the relationship to selected lyrics. Include intro/fade/outro within the 30-second total.

Create both outputs:

- 1080 x 1920, 9:16 social reel: a focused excerpt, with appropriate framing and readable text.
- 1920 x 1080, 16:9 YouTube version: normally the complete supplied song when approximately 2-4 minutes, with visual progression through stanzas and musical movement.

If the supplied audio is too short or long for the requested band, resolve whether to use its natural duration, make an intentional musical edit or await an extended song. Do not auto-repeat sung verses, accelerate/slow the song or append unrelated music just to meet a length.

Decide between readable lyric phrases and full captions based on the brief; preserve a clean master and editable text.
